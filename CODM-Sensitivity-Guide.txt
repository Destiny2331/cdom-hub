================================
TIPS
================================

1. Test your sensitivity in Practice Range.
2. Increase sensitivity if turning feels too slow.
3. Decrease sensitivity if your aim feels shaky.
4. Keep your FPS as high and stable as possible.
5. Give yourself time to adjust before changing
   your settings again.
