WEAPON GUIDES
==============================

🔫 AK117

Best for:
• Medium-range fights
• Fast movement
• Easy recoil control

Tips:
• Use a stable build.
• Control your recoil while firing.
• Good for both MP and BR.


🔫 KRM-262

Best for:
• Close-range fights
• Aggressive players

Tips:
• Get close before firing.
• Aim for the upper body.
• Practice movement and slide shots.


🔫 QQ9

Best for:
• Fast close-range combat
• Rush gameplay

Tips:
• Keep moving while fighting.
• Use ADS when needed.
• Control the recoil at medium range.


🔫 CBR4

Best for:
• Multiplayer
• Close and medium range

Tips:
• Good for aggressive gameplay.
• Use a balanced attachment setup.
• Keep your aim around the upper chest.


🔫 DL Q33

Best for:
• Sniping
• Long-range fights

Tips:
• Find a good position.
• Keep your crosshair at head level.
• Don't stay exposed after firing.


🎮 GENERAL WEAPON TIPS

1. Practice every weapon before using it in ranked.
2. Choose attachments that match your play style.
3. Keep your sensitivity consistent.
4. Learn recoil patterns.
5. Use movement while fighting.
6. Practice in the Training Range.
